Events Poster Maker

Add a section, pick its month and year, then add its events. The poster on the right updates as you type. Leave Day blank and it's worked out from the month and date. Drag ⋮⋮ to move an event or a whole section.
Card colours are automatic: navy for the current month, blue for upcoming months, gold for every-week events, teal for Thanksgiving and red for Blood Donation.
